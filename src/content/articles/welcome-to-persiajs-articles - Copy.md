## Why a hub?

Community writing used to live on LinkedIn and in Telegram. Neither is easy to search, and neither is indexed well by search engines. This site gives every article a permanent, fast, searchable home.

## How to contribute

1. Write your article in Markdown.
2. Add it to the `articles` collection with a short English slug.
3. Share the link on LinkedIn and Telegram.
